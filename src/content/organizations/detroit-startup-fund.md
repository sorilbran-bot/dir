name: Detroit Startup Fund
kind: funder
website: https://detroitmi.gov/startup
summary: A City of Detroit grant fund for Detroit-based tech startups. It gives $15,000 seed grants and $50,000 scale grants and is paid for from the City's general fund.
criteria:
  source_label: Detroit Startup Fund Program Guidelines (DEGA)
  source_url: https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/2025-07/DEGA%20Approved%20Detroit%20Startup%20Fund%20Program%20Guidelines.pdf
  source_date: July 2025, marked "pending approval by the DEGA Executive Committee"
  captured: 2026-11-18
  last_checked: 2026-11-18
  location_rule: The startup's headquarters must be in the City of Detroit, or the startup must have a real, ongoing operational presence there (for example, two or more team members or a core founder working from Detroit). For seed grants only, an out-of-state startup can qualify by signing a lease to move to Detroit within three months, and the grant isn't paid until it moves.
  location_proof: Proof of a Detroit business address at application, or a relocation agreement for out-of-state startups.
  other:
    - A for-profit, legally incorporated technology startup established no more than 10 years ago.
    - A scalable, venture-backable product with potential civic use or benefit to Detroit residents.
    - City business certification (CRIO), or a commitment to get it before the grant is paid.
    - A commitment to share progress data with DEGC and the City.
    - "Scale grants only: at least $100,000 in outside private investment in the past 18 months, and actively raising at least $250,000. Grants and prizes don't count toward this."
  note: DIR found no separate guidelines for later rounds and treats these as current until the program publishes new ones.
