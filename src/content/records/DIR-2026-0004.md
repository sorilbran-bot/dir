The Detroit Startup Fund awarded 1st Step Healthcare Training a $15,000 seed grant in Round 3. The City of Detroit and DEGC presented 13 grants at Vander Detroit on November 16, 2026. 1st Step Healthcare Training is a state-approved health care training provider offering CNA, phlebotomy and EKG certification. 1st Step Healthcare Training has not published its own announcement yet.

*DIR attended the awards ceremony at the invitation of the City of Detroit.*
