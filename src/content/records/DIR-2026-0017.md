Hurley's Coffee & Mercantile received the $1,500 Audience Choice award, chosen by attendee vote, at the Lake Trust Foundation's Entrepreneur Pitch Competition on November 5, 2026, at Lake Trust Credit Union's headquarters in Brighton. Nine entrepreneurs who completed the Lake Trust Entrepreneurship Institute pitched for $20,000 in prizes.
