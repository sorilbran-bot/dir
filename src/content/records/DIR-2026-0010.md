The Detroit Startup Fund awarded Ultrium Technologies a $15,000 seed grant in Round 3. The City of Detroit and DEGC presented 13 grants at Vander Detroit on November 16, 2026. Ultrium Technologies builds a repair intelligence platform for independent auto shops. Ultrium Technologies announced the grant on LinkedIn.

*DIR attended the awards ceremony at the invitation of the City of Detroit.*
