NextCycle Michigan selected ESSPI, listed in Detroit, as one of nine teams in its EV Battery Recycling & Reuse cohort, announced November 10, 2026. ESSPI makes a platform that detects lithium-ion battery failures in storage before they cause fires, and is seeking formal agreements with Michigan recyclers. The six-month accelerator, funded by the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE), ends with a live pitch competition in spring 2027.
