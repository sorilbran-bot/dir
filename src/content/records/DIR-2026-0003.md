The Detroit Startup Fund awarded Neumo a $50,000 scale grant in Round 3. The City of Detroit and DEGC presented 13 grants at Vander Detroit on November 16, 2026. Neumo builds contactless brain-sensing technology that detects driver drowsiness and distraction. Neumo also received a $15,000 seed grant in Round 1, which makes it the only company to win both a seed and a scale grant from the fund. Neumo has not published its own announcement yet.

*DIR attended the awards ceremony at the invitation of the City of Detroit.*
