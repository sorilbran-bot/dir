The Detroit Startup Fund awarded Clixie AI a $50,000 scale grant in Round 3. The City of Detroit and DEGC presented 13 grants at Vander Detroit on November 16, 2026. Clixie AI builds an AI learning platform for employee training, licensing, certification and continuing education. Clixie AI has not published its own announcement yet.

*DIR attended the awards ceremony at the invitation of the City of Detroit.*
