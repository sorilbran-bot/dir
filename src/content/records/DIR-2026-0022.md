NextCycle Michigan selected Great Lakes Recycling of River Rouge as one of nine teams in its EV Battery Recycling & Reuse cohort, announced November 10, 2026. The company is working to scale EV battery dismantling through scrap yards and partnerships with automakers. The six-month accelerator, funded by the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE), ends with a live pitch competition in spring 2027.
