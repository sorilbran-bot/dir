The Detroit Startup Fund awarded AmHyTech a $50,000 scale grant in Round 3. The City of Detroit and DEGC presented 13 grants at Vander Detroit on November 16, 2026. AmHyTech is commercializing a way to store and move ammonia without high-pressure tanks or deep refrigeration. AmHyTech has not published its own announcement yet.

*DIR attended the awards ceremony at the invitation of the City of Detroit.*
