The ReVamp Clothing Swap Festival pitched at the NextCycle Michigan Fall 2026 Showcase on November 10, 2026, at the Kellogg Hotel & Conference Center in East Lansing. It took both the $5,000 Best Pitch award and the $2,500 People's Choice award in the Recycling Supply Chain Solutions track. Like each of the 16 teams that finished the accelerator, it also received a $5,000 Harvest Grant from the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE). NextCycle Michigan published the results on November 20.
