1st Step Healthcare Training took first place and $10,000 at the Lake Trust Foundation's Entrepreneur Pitch Competition on November 5, 2026, at Lake Trust Credit Union's headquarters in Brighton. Nine entrepreneurs who completed the Lake Trust Entrepreneurship Institute pitched for $20,000 in prizes. 1st Step Healthcare Training is a state-approved school offering CNA training.
