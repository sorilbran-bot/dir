Recycle Here! pitched at the NextCycle Michigan Fall 2026 Showcase on November 10, 2026, at the Kellogg Hotel & Conference Center in East Lansing. It took the $5,000 Best Pitch award in the Public-Led Solutions track. Like each of the 16 teams that finished the accelerator, it also received a $5,000 Harvest Grant from the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE). NextCycle Michigan published the results on November 20.
