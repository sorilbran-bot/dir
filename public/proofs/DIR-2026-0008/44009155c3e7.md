The Detroit Startup Fund awarded Lite Raise a $15,000 seed grant in Round 3. The City of Detroit and DEGC presented 13 grants at Vander Detroit on November 16, 2026. Lite Raise makes fundraising, donor management and compliance software for nonprofits. Lite Raise has not published its own announcement yet.

*DIR attended the awards ceremony at the invitation of the City of Detroit.*
