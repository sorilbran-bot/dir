John and Allyson Smith held the grand opening of their Beyond Juicery + Eatery franchise at 17149 Livernois Ave on Saturday, November 21, 2026. The fast-casual spot serves smoothies, wraps, salads and snacks, about a block from the University of Detroit Mercy's McNichols Campus. It is the first Black-owned Beyond Juicery + Eatery in Detroit. Allyson Smith is a Detroit Mercy alum.
