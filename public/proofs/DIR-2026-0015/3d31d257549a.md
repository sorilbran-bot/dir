Little Susie Café & Sweetshop took second place and $5,000 at the Lake Trust Foundation's Entrepreneur Pitch Competition on November 5, 2026, at Lake Trust Credit Union's headquarters in Brighton. Nine entrepreneurs who completed the Lake Trust Entrepreneurship Institute pitched for $20,000 in prizes. Lake Trust describes Little Susie Café & Sweetshop as a neighborhood cafe planning to offer sandwiches, paninis and soups. Its website says it is opening in Detroit in 2027.
