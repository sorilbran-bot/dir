The Detroit Startup Fund awarded The Heart Next Door a $15,000 seed grant in Round 3. The City of Detroit and DEGC presented 13 grants at Vander Detroit on November 16, 2026. The Heart Next Door runs a maternal health technology platform. The Heart Next Door has not published its own announcement yet.

*DIR attended the awards ceremony at the invitation of the City of Detroit.*
